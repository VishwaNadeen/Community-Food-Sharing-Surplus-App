# Contributing to Community Food Sharing Surplus App

Thank you for contributing to the Community Food Sharing Surplus App project. This project was developed collaboratively by a team of four members, with each member responsible for specific features and functionalities.

## Team Members and Contributions

### 1. Vishwa Nadeen (HPVN Pathirana)
**Responsibilities: Project Setup, Authentication, and Recipient Module**

- Set up and configured the project structure.
- Implemented role-based login functionality.
- Developed user login and profile management features.
- Developed the Recipient side of the application, including recipient-related functionalities.

### 2. Methma Sankalpani (Sankaalpani GM)
**Responsibilities: Volunteer and Driver Module**

- Developed the Volunteer and Driver side of the application.
- Implemented features related to volunteer and driver activities.
- Worked on the functionalities required to support food delivery and distribution.

### 3. K. D. S. Nimesha
**Responsibilities: Donor Module**

- Developed the Donor side of the application.
- Implemented donor-related features and functionalities.
- Worked on the features that support food donation management.

### 4. S. P. T. Lakmini
**Responsibilities: NGO Module**

- Developed the NGO side of the application.
- Implemented NGO-related features and functionalities.
- Worked on features that support NGO activities and food distribution coordination.

## Team Collaboration

All four team members contributed to developing the Community Food Sharing Surplus App. Each member focused on their assigned module, helping to build an integrated application that supports food donation, NGO coordination, recipient access, and volunteer delivery management.
